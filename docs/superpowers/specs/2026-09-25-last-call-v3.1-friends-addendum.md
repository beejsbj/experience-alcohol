# Last Call v3.1 — friends write on it

Date: 2026-09-25
Amends `2026-09-25-last-call-v3-design.md` after the first round of feedback.

## The idea, restated

It's receipt paper, and a group of friends are writing all over it. That's
where the pen, the arrows and the doodles come from. Anything that isn't the
printer or a friend's pen (rubber stamps) goes.

## Changes

### Nothing assumed about a new face (`IntroFlow.vue`)

`buildPerson` defaults still exist for the math, but a fresh receipt
(`needsIntro: true`, set by `createSession` and `addPerson`) asks first, one
question at a time, written down the paper in the new person's pen:

1. **who's this tab for?** — the name, written on (`WriteOn`, focuses itself).
2. **for the math — which body?** — female / male with hand-drawn glyphs
   (`SexGlyph.vue`), circled on tap. A printed line explains why it's asked.
3. **roughly how heavy?** — a printed ruler (`WeightRuler.vue`, 40–140 kg) with
   a pen mark you drag; kg in pen, ≈ lb in print.
4. **your pen:** — handed over at random from the inks nobody at the table is
   holding (`pickPen`); tap the squiggle for another. **cheers →** finishes
   (`store.introduce`). Skipping the name gives `guest <seat>`.

The bar mat stays away until the questions are answered. Existing people
(no `needsIntro` field) are never re-asked.

### No stamps

- Pace verdict → `VerdictNote.vue`: "on pace" with a tick, "easy now…" with a
  wave, "slow down!" boxed, "cut off." ringed and double-underlined.
- Table view scraps use the same notes; the keepsake's PAID is a red-pen
  "paid!" ringed with "thanks all x"; the napkin's button is
  "put it on the mat →", underlined.
- `StampVerdict.vue`, `.stamp` and `stamp-thump` are deleted.

### Handwritten personal details

Body (glyph) and weight are written in the person's pen on the receipt; tap
either to change it.

### The underline under the feeling (`FeelingUnderline.vue`)

A family of pen underlines (`underlineStyle` / `underlinePath` in
`utils/doodles.js`): swash or double when sober, wave when relaxed, loops when
tipsy, zigzag/scribble further in; wobble scales with BAC. Seeded per person
and state. Tap the feeling word to have another go.

### Friends' doodles and notes (`utils/doodles.js`, `Doodle.vue`)

- A library of pen doodles (star, heart, smiley, spiral, sparkle, bolt,
  flower, note, sun, crown, bubbles, squiggle, cheers, asterisk, arrows) and
  handwritten words (xo, <3, !!, ha!, yay, wooo, cheers, 10/10, hi :)),
  drawn with a seeded tremor.
- `friendMarks`: eight slots on each receipt. Two marks from the start, more
  as the whole table pours; never the same doodle twice on one receipt; each
  in a friend's ink (your own if you're alone). Marks already down never
  move.
- `friendNote`: one signed note from someone at the table, written over the
  chart's headroom with an arrow, fitted to the pace ("sip, don't gulp",
  "hey. water. now.", "taxi's on us"…). Alone, it's signed "— the bar".

### The napkin: sensible defaults (`NapkinSlip.vue`, `VesselIcon.vue`)

"what's it in?" — pint, mug, can, bottle, wine, bubbly, highball, on the rocks
(`VESSELS`) — circling one fills in its usual pour and strength. Rough
strengths (`STRENGTHS`: light 4 … spirit 40) can be circled too; the numbers
stay tap-to-edit. Naming is optional (falls back to the vessel). The vessel is
kept on the custom drink.

### More on the mat (`GlassTopDown.vue`, `PourMat.vue`)

New top-down drinks: mug (with handle), can (pull tab; comes back sealed when
it's time), bottle (crown cap; uncapped while you wait), flute (bubbles),
highball (lime wedge, straw, ice). When there are more drinks than fit, the
mat slides sideways under your thumb (edges fade), a drag never pours, and a
new house special slides into view. The napkin is pinned at the right end.

### Sharing the table (`RoomSlip.vue`, `parseRoomCode`)

- Solo, the coaster asks: **open this table to friends →** or **sit at a
  friend's table ↻**, which turns the coaster over to write in their code
  (or paste their link). `parseRoomCode` accepts any case, spaces, missing
  dash, or the whole link.
- At a table: QR, the code in dot matrix (tap to copy) and on the rim, the
  link printed underneath with **copy link** / **send it →**.
- Each receipt says in pen whose phone is holding it.

### Fix: taps on the paper

The pile captured the pointer on touch-down, which retargets the eventual
click to the card — so taps on anything on the receipt (the vibe circles,
the pen swap, the intro) could be lost. Capture now starts only once the
finger has moved past the drag threshold.
